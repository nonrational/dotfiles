New Mac? Open Terminal and run:

curl -sS https://nonration.al/setup.sh | bash

It stops at three checkpoints and tells you what to do at each one. Re-run the same command after each:

  1. Finish the Command Line Tools installer.
  2. Grant your terminal Full Disk Access, then restart it.
  3. Let the Mac reboot, then run it once more.

The script and everything it installs live at nonrational/dotfiles.
