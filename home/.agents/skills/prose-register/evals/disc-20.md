Which opening is on-register, and which SKILL.md rule decides it? This is a structural judgment, not a sentence-level one: evaluate the whole opening move. Both are the first paragraph of the same essay, about an agent that writes under its owner's name.

## rule

Open with substance. Open on the state of play, not on another writer's take; a cited author is a reference in the first paragraph, never the hook.

## cited_author

Niklas Gruhn put the objection in a title: "Don't be a meat proxy." His complaint is the reply that is plainly an AI's answer, relayed by a person who added nothing. "By all means, prompt AI. But don't just relay the output." Read it, understand it, validate it, then answer in your own words.

## state_of_play

Everyone has an agent in their pocket now, which makes it easy to conjure words from nothing that look like something. Niklas Gruhn named the failure in a title: "Don't be a meat proxy." He's right: we've all done it, and we all detest it.
